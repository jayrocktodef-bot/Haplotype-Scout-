# Haplotype Scout — Studio Edition

> ⚠️ **Beta — Research & Educational Exploration.** Haplotype Scout is an exploratory phylogenetic studio designed for high-resolution Y-DNA & mtDNA haplogroup deconvolution. It operates 100% client-side in your web browser.

**Haplotype Scout** is part of the **Written In The Genome** genomic application suite. It provides end-to-end non-autosomal phylogenetic analysis, high-resolution ISOGG/PhyloTree lineage inference, molecular coalescence dating (TMRCA), branch mutation ladder verification, physical relief paleomigration mapping, and direct cladistic matching against sequenced ancient archaeological specimens.

## ✨ Key Features

1. **High-Resolution DAG Lineage Inference**:
   - Comprehensive traversal of the Non-Recombining Y-Chromosome (NRY) and Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) phylogenetic trees.
   - Exact diagnostic SNP verification with transversion weighting and link disequilibrium (LD) proxy imputations.

2. **Evolutionary Coalescence & TMRCA Clock**:
   - Calibrated molecular clock equations ($\mu_{\text{Y}} \approx 8.71 \times 10^{-10} \text{ mut/bp/yr}$, $\mu_{\text{MT}} \approx 1\text{ mut}/2,700\text{ yrs}$).
   - Computes formed age, TMRCA years Before Present (BP), calendar BCE/CE dates, and 95% Poisson confidence intervals aligned with human archaeological epoch horizons.

3. **Phylogenetic Branch Mutation Ladder Audit & Private Variant Caller**:
   - Node-by-node audit along your ancestral phylogenetic spine ($+ / - / ?$).
   - Detects homoplasic back-mutations and surfaces candidate novel/private variants downstream of terminal clades.

4. **Paleogeographic Steppe & Clan Migration Map**:
   - Physical relief topography, elevation shading, and ocean bathymetric ridges.
   - 100,000-year clan trajectory with toggleable **Last Glacial Maximum (LGM)** ice sheets and **Doggerland / Beringia** prehistoric land bridges.

5. **Ancient Archaeological Sample Matcher**:
   - Cladistic proximity scoring against iconic sequenced ancient human remains (*Ötzi the Iceman, Cheddar Man, Yamnaya Steppe Pastoralists, Viking Sigtuna, Clovis Anzick-1, Taforalt, and Tianyuan*).

6. **Archaic Hominin Introgression & Phased Microhaplotypes**:
   - Neanderthal (*Vindija 33.19*) and Denisovan (*Denisova 3*) admixture percentage estimation and functional evolutionary loci inspector (*EPAS1, BNC2, OAS1, STAT2*).
   - 100-block phased microhaplotype ancestral cluster deconvolution.

7. **Interactive SVG Phylogenetic Tree Viewer**:
   - Full-screen zoomable and pannable Y-DNA & mtDNA DAG tree with live path tracing from Root to your terminal clade.
